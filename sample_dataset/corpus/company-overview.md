# Lumen Playables: Company Overview

Lumen Playables is a fictional interactive ad studio founded in 2019, headquartered in Lisbon with a remote production team across Europe. The studio builds HTML5 playable ads for mobile game and app clients.

The company is organized into three production pods (Nova, Orbit, Flare), each with a producer, two developers and a designer. A separate Platform team owns the build pipeline, the Lumen SDK and network compliance. Client communication runs through producers; the Platform team is never client-facing.

Lumen delivers around 60 playables per month. Standard delivery time for a new concept is 10 business days from brief approval; iterations on an existing playable are 3 business days.
