# Live Touring and Performance Contracts Guide

## Overview

Live performances and touring represent a significant income stream for performing artists. Negotiating live performance contracts requires clear terms covering financial compensation, technical sound riders, hospitality requirements, venue safety, and performance royalty reporting.

## Key Deal Structures in Live Performance Agreements

Live booking compensation structures generally fall into four primary categories:

1. **Flat Guarantee**: The venue/promoter pays a fixed fee (e.g., $10,000) regardless of ticket sales or attendance.
2. **Door Split**: The artist receives a percentage (e.g., 70% to 85%) of ticket sales revenue collected at the door, with no guaranteed minimum payment.
3. **Guarantee Plus Percentage (Plus Deal)**: The artist receives a guaranteed flat fee plus a percentage (e.g., 85%) of net ticket proceeds once the venue breaks even on production costs.
4. **Guarantee Versus Percentage (Versus Deal)**: The artist receives whichever amount is higher: the flat guarantee fee OR a specified percentage of gross/net ticket sales.

## Technical Rider & Sound Specifications

The **Technical Rider** forms an enforceable annex to the performance contract, outlining mandatory production equipment:

- **FOH (Front of House) Console**: Minimum 32-channel digital mixing console (e.g., DiGiCo SD9, Avid S6L, Allen & Heath dLive) with dedicated multitrack recording outputs.
- **Monitoring System**: In-Ear Monitor (IEM) wireless transmitters (e.g., Shure PSM1000) operating on clean RF frequency bands, alongside side-fill stage wedges.
- **Stage Plot & Input List**: Clear spatial layout showing instrument placement, vocal mic positions, D.I. box locations, and AC power drop requirements.

## Force Majeure and Cancellation Clauses

- **Force Majeure**: Excuses performance obligations in the event of unpredictable disasters (e.g., extreme weather, venue destruction, government restrictions).
- **Cancellation Notice & Deposit Retention**:
  - Deposit: Venue must pay a 50% non-refundable deposit upon contract execution.
  - If the venue cancels within 30 days of the performance date, the artist retains the 50% deposit.
  - If the venue cancels within 7 days of the performance, the full 100% contract guarantee becomes immediately due.

## PRO Live Performance Royalty Reporting

Songwriters are entitled to collect live performance royalties whenever their songs are played live at licensed venues or festivals.
- Artists must submit their **Setlists** (song titles, writers, and performance dates) to their respective PRO portals (ASCAP OnStage, BMI Live, PRS Performers).
- Major festival stages pay premium live performance royalty allocations based on venue capacity and ticket pricing.
