# Onboarding: New Developer

Week 1: environment setup, lumen-build access, and a shadow delivery with your pod. Every new developer rebuilds one past playable from its brief as a training exercise; the result is reviewed by the pod's senior developer, not delivered.

Week 2: first real ticket, limited to an iteration on an existing playable (3-day scope). New developers do not own a new-concept delivery before their fourth week.

Access: repository and CI access come from the Platform team; client folders are producer-granted per project. The staging CDN is open to all developers; production upload rights are limited to producers and the Platform team.
