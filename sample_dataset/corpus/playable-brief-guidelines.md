# Writing a Playable Brief

Every playable starts from a brief approved by the client. A valid brief must contain: the core loop to showcase, the target audience, the tone, visual style references, required end-card call to action, and any network-specific constraints.

Two rules are non-negotiable at Lumen:

1. The first interaction must be reachable within 3 seconds of load. If the intro runs longer, it must be skippable.
2. Tutorials are shown, not written. Use pointer hands and highlights; text instructions are limited to one short line at a time.

Briefs that request more than two distinct mechanics in a single playable are escalated to the pod producer, because multi-mechanic playables historically underperform and blow the size budget.
